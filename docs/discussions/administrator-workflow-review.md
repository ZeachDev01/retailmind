## Confirmed decisions

- Store Overview is the Administrator's daily starting point for Store-wide attention, including GCash Service issues. Detailed investigation and actions remain in their dedicated workspaces under existing permissions.
- Keep Store Overview clean: add one GCash row within Needs attention, with separate unresolved-exchange and unwaived-closing-discrepancy counts linking to their respective review tabs. Counts cover all dates and Cashiers independently of reporting filters. Hide the row when both counts are zero; show an unavailable state when alerts cannot load.
- GCash funds, service fees, and exchange activity remain in GCash Overview. Do not add these summaries, cards, or charts to Store Overview or combine them with Product sales measures.
- A cash-variance Review link must identify the exact Cashier Shift and expose expected cash, counted cash, variance, Cashier, and closing notes.
- The Administrator may Mark reviewed with a required explanation. Retain reviewer identity and time, remove the reviewed item from pending attention, and preserve original amounts and history. Review does not claim that missing money was recovered.
- The Administrator may save investigation notes without clearing the alert. Mark reviewed is available for a finished investigation, not merely opening the record or saving a note.
- Ordinary cash variances that reach the review threshold should say Needs review. Reserve Critical for explicitly agreed urgent conditions; those conditions are not yet defined.
- A finished investigation may be marked reviewed even when money remains missing. The required explanation must clearly record that the shortage remains; review must not imply repayment or change the original amounts.
- Apply one cash-review threshold rule to Dashboard alerts and Shift closing: a nonzero shortage or excess whose absolute amount equals or exceeds the configured Store limit requires review. Smaller differences remain visible in Cashier Shift reports without adding Dashboard alerts. PHP 100 was an example, not a newly chosen limit.
- A correctly applied, approved Promotion must not create a Needs attention item solely because its discount is large. Keep that discount visible in sales reporting. The later confirmed decision below retires size-only discount alerts entirely.
- Normal pending Stock Issues belong under Store status, with their count and the label Waiting for Inventory Manager. Do not put them in Needs attention as an Administrator approval task. Administrator oversight remains read-only; the Inventory Manager decides the issues.
- Retire size-only Unusual discount Dashboard items, exception counts, and notifications. Supported checkout only permits eligible Administrator-created Promotions, so discount size alone does not establish an unresolved problem. Preserve discounts in receipts and reports, including historical manual/legacy records; missing Promotion attribution alone does not prove wrongdoing. Do not introduce a replacement discount-detection workflow.
- Before Fiscal Period closure, show the unfinished work required by the confirmed fiscal-period policy and links to the relevant work: open Cashier Shifts, open or unreconciled GCash sessions, and pending Inventory Counts within the period. Keep closure blocked until prerequisites are met and explain what must happen next. Preserve existing role boundaries when directing work to another role.
- Fiscal Period closing confirmation must explain that new sales need an open period covering their date. After closing, show Open next period with its start date set to the day after the previous period ends. The Administrator chooses the end date and explicitly confirms opening; do not automatically close or open periods.
- For a failed GCash exchange, explain that Verify & Clear confirms completion and is not a dismissal action. Direct the responsible Cashier to the existing failed-exchange handling in the Cashier Workspace; keep the exchange pending until properly handled. An Administrator with assigned Cashier access may switch Workspace under existing controls. Do not add Administrator cancellation authority.
- Before finishing a GCash closing-discrepancy review covering cash and wallet, require both differences to be checked and explained. Keep the alert while either remains unchecked. Do not offset a cash shortage against wallet excess; preserve both original amounts and the review history.
- Rename the GCash closing-discrepancy action Waive to Mark reviewed. Explain that the Administrator checked the cash and wallet differences and saved an explanation; original amounts remain in history, and missing money is not thereby returned. Preserve the existing accountable approval/audit behavior rather than treating review as a cash adjustment.

In the Super Administrator Workspace, Store oversight is read-only: GCash approvals and other Store mutations require switching to the corresponding operational Workspace with that role already assigned. Assigned roles do not combine permissions in the active Workspace. ## Cross-workflow consistency notes

- Accepted follow-up responsibility survives Shift/session closure; it does not require keeping live custody open. Count/close GCash custody, close Product Shifts, finalize/open periods, then require the responsible Cashier's new eligible Shift/session for later actual movements.
- Preserve original carryover counts as historical evidence, but determine currently outstanding obligations from actual linked exchange state rather than treating an old count as permanent pending truth.
- Snapshot/count-only GCash closure must remain available when money posting is blocked, as already required by the fiscal-period specification. Actual repayment/stock movements still require a valid covering period; the existing controlled amendment of an Open period's end date supplies the approved cleanup route if needed.
- A shortage does not establish a person's debt. Reviewing a shortage and carrying an independently evidenced customer obligation are different acts.
- Money linked to both an exchange and a discrepancy is one actual movement with multiple traceable links, never two receipts of the same cash.
- Fees charged use completion date; fee returns use actual return date. Fees after returns may legitimately be negative for a selected period; do not silently clamp the result to zero.
- Ordinary role removal immediately withdraws that Workspace and revokes sessions while preserving unfinished operational work. Revoke Sessions means sign out current sessions; it does not disable the account, prohibit signing in again, or close cash work.
- Preserve Holiday's existing closed-trading-day meaning for forecasting; when trading on a holiday, use Open. Clarify labels without adding independent holiday/open dimensions.
- Retain the existing exchange CSV, clearly labelled exchange-only. The Money history view does not by itself authorize a new complete-ledger export feature.
- Supervisor prompts must preserve existing account/password-state checks and enforce appropriate existing authentication-abuse protections without logging in or switching the approving person's session as a side effect.
- Preserve ADR-0019's read-only remote-monitoring boundary, including restrictions on remote approvals, backups, and bulk exports. An assigned Administrator role does not override this independent access boundary.

## Cash review workflow

1. Show Needs review on Store Overview when a closed Cashier Shift meets the agreed cash-difference rule.
2. Review opens that exact Shift even when it falls outside the report's default date range.
3. Show the Cashier, register, Shift dates, expected cash, counted cash, cash difference, and closing notes. Explain a negative difference as money short and a positive difference as extra money.
4. The Administrator checks the records and asks the Cashier what happened.
5. Save investigation notes while checks continue. Keep the item in Needs attention.
6. When the investigation is finished, Mark reviewed requires an explanation and records the reviewer and time. Keep the original cash amounts and previous investigation notes.
7. Remove the reviewed item from pending attention while keeping its review and original difference available in history.

Steps about investigation and completion reflect the confirmed choices. A finished review may retain an unrecovered shortage, explicitly described in its explanation. Preserve earlier investigation notes rather than silently replacing them with the final explanation. Do not interpret Mark reviewed as repayment, a cash adjustment, or permission to deduct staff wages.
