Short demo route

  1. Inventory Manager: Inventory Overview. Identify one active product, its code, price, and sellable stock.
  2. Stock Receiving: show an approved purchasing reference; record a small delivery in a separate demo environment. Confirm accepted stock increased.
  3. Cashier Shifts: open an available Register with counted opening cash.
  4. Point of Sale: scan or type the code, explain the cart, complete a small cash sale, show the receipt.
  5. Inventory Overview and Sales Transactions: confirm the stock deduction and recorded sale.
  6. Cashier Shifts: reconcile counted cash with expected cash; resolve Held Sales before closing.
  7. Inventory Manager and Administrator: explain request → approval → delivery. Show forecasting only when its data source and setup are verified.

What changes inventory?

EventInventory effect
Scan / hold cartNo completed-sale deduction.
Successful checkoutDeduct sold units.
Accepted deliveryAdd accepted units, excluding damaged delivery units.
Pending Stock IssueNo deduction yet.
Approved Stock IssueDeduct approved units.
RefundAdd only Restockable returned units.
Saved inventory countReconcile recorded stock to counted stock.
Forecast / purchasing approvalNo stock movement from the estimate or approval itself.

Cash and terminology

Expected cash = opening cash + cash sales + cash additions − payouts − safe drops − cash refunds − applicable approved legacy cash reversals. Compare against physical cash; card and e-wallet sales do not add drawer cash. Calculation source.

Shift: accountable drawer session. Register: physical till. Sale Receipt: completed customer purchase record. Stock Issue: report awaiting inventory review. Demand Forecast: estimated units for 7, 14, or 28 days. Replenishment: reviewed restocking work.

Before the visit

  • Use clearly labeled demo records in a separate demo environment; rehearsal transactions affect reports, stock, and history.
  • Check operational accounts, available Register, open Fiscal Period, product codes, price, and nonexpired sellable stock.
  • Try the actual scanner and receipt printer. Keep typed-code entry ready.
  • Verify non-cash payment externally before recording its reference; RetailMind does not transfer card or e-wallet funds.
  • Do not promise automatic phone-to-laptop cart handoff, offline checkout, or Store forecast accuracy without verifying that behavior.

Learn from the client

Ask: “How do you record deliveries? How do you track tingi or sachet units? How do you handle damaged stock and customer returns? Who closes the cash drawer? Do you need utang tracking?” Treat the answers as requirements to assess, not features already established.
