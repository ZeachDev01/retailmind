# Administrator workflow alignment and accountable corrections

Specification synthesized on 2026-10-27 Philippine time from the confirmed Administrator workflow review through Q59. Test seams confirmed in this specification session. Publication organizes future implementation; this session does not authorize application implementation or live operations.

Published as [GitHub issue #326](https://github.com/ZeachDev01/retailmind/issues/326), labelled `ready-for-agent`.

## Problem Statement

The Administrator needs to understand what requires attention, investigate the exact records, and direct unfinished work to the right person. Current workflows include dead-end alerts, misleading review labels, inconsistent cash thresholds, incomplete GCash follow-up, and unclear consequences for approvals, account changes, reporting, and recovery.

These ambiguities can make a reviewed shortage look recovered, a cancelled exchange look repaid, a closed session look like a settled customer obligation, or an approved correction look executed. Role bypasses and unclear financial measures further weaken accountability. The Store needs clear, consistent workflows that preserve original evidence and distinguish investigation, authorization, responsibility, and actual money or stock movement.

## Solution

Make Store Overview the Administrator's daily starting point for actionable Store-wide attention, with investigation and execution in their dedicated Workspaces. Give reviews explicit explanations and history, keep unsettled obligations visible across handover and closure, and require actual movement evidence before showing repayment or correction as complete.

Align supervisor approval, staff lifecycle, Fiscal Period guidance, GCash controls, refund corrections, reporting, settings, forecasts, and protected restore guidance with the accepted role boundaries. Preserve immutable records, original attribution, existing safeguards, and the single-Store model. Clearly identify unavailable information and the next permitted action.

## Implementation Decisions

- Reuse the existing StoreOperationsDashboardWorkspace, Attention services, CashierShiftService, StoreShiftReportService, GCashTransactionService, CashRefundService, RefundExceptionService, FiscalPeriodGuardService, authorization policy/context, ProtectedAuditRecordService, reporting services, and protected backup/recovery components. Extend current application and request boundaries; do not add a parallel workflow engine, shared test facade, or dependency.
- Reach affected callers, UI, routes, service queries, notifications, cached attention aggregates, filters, exports, settings consumers, fixtures, and schema support. A hidden button is insufficient authorization. Preserve validation, CSRF protection, appropriate authentication-abuse controls, Store write gates, and existing transaction/error handling. Implementation must inspect every affected caller before changing a shared contract.
- Store Overview receives one GCash Needs attention row with distinct unresolved-exchange and unreviewed-closing-difference counts and tab-specific destinations. Its counts are Store-wide across all dates/Cashiers; report filters must not narrow them. Hide only when both are known zero. Preserve honest loading/failure states and refresh after successful actions. GCash balances, fees, and activity stay in GCash Overview and outside Product sales measures.
- Cash variance attention must carry the exact Shift identity into the review destination, independent of default date filters. Show Cashier, Register, open/close dates, expected and counted cash, signed difference, and closing notes. Explain negative as short and positive as extra. Use Needs review for ordinary qualifying differences; no new Critical conditions are defined.
- Apply a shared closure rule: a nonzero difference requires review when its absolute amount equals or exceeds the configured Store limit. Retain smaller differences in reports. PHP 100 is an example, not a selected limit. Persist the threshold and reason applicable at closure so Store/platform limit changes affect future closures without clearing previously required reviews.
- Save investigation notes independently of completion and preserve them alongside the final explanation. Mark reviewed requires a finished investigation, explanation, reviewer, and time; it removes pending attention while retaining original amounts and all history. Explicitly record any remaining shortage. Review is not recovery, debt assignment, a cash adjustment, or permission for wage deduction. Reopen review requires a reason, restores attention, and preserves all earlier notes, reviewers, times, and conclusions. Apply reopening to the cash-review workflows covered by the confirmed decision without inventing a general reopening mechanism for unrelated records.
- Remove size-only Unusual discount attention, related exception/comparison counts, notifications, and obsolete settings/references. Preserve Promotion discounts and manual/legacy discount history in receipts/reports; absent Promotion attribution is not proof of wrongdoing. Remove Legacy Reversal alerts, retired-tab links, and related exception counts without deleting underlying records or recreating reversal UI. No historical migration/review workflow is required for this development cleanup.
- Normal pending Stock Issues belong in Store status with Waiting for Inventory Manager and their count. Retain existing configured overdue/high-value oversight alerts labelled Follow up with Inventory Manager. Administrator oversight stays read-only; the Inventory Manager decides inventory effects. Verify consumers before removing persisted settings, remove ineffective controls rather than manufacturing behavior, and explain each remaining control's actual effect while retaining unrelated validation.
- Reuse the accepted Fiscal Period lifecycle and commit-time safeguards. Present open Shifts, open/unreconciled GCash sessions, and in-period pending Inventory Counts as actionable blockers with role-correct links. Preserve all other prerequisites. Closing confirmation explains that new sales need a covering open period; after closing offer Open next period starting the next day, with an Administrator-selected end date and explicit confirmation. Never roll periods automatically.
- Clarify that GCash custody reconciliation differs from exchange settlement. An unsettled exchange may survive Fiscal Period closure only when physical cash/wallet have been counted and checked, the session otherwise closes properly, and the obligation is explicitly carried forward with a responsible Cashier. Preserve original period/session attribution, pending state, and owed amount. Determine current obligations from linked exchange state rather than freezing an old carryover count as permanent truth. A shortage alone does not establish a person's debt.
- Accepted follow-up responsibility survives Shift/session/period closure without keeping custody open. Preserve snapshot/count-only session closure when money posting is blocked. Later actual money or stock movements still require a valid covering open period and the responsible operator's eligible Shift/session; use the existing controlled amendment of an open period's end date when needed. Do not silently backdate later payments or relax fiscal protection.
- Verify & Clear confirms completed exchange evidence and posts only applicable remaining movements; it is not dismissal. Failed handling remains in the responsible Cashier workflow, reachable by eligible assigned Workspace switching. Administrator cancellation authority is not added. Completion may rely on responsible Cashier checks recording who checked cash/wallet and what each found, with Administrator accountability and existing confirmations. Either uncertain part keeps the exchange pending; expected balances alone are insufficient.
- Rename the GCash discrepancy user action Waive to Mark reviewed while preserving existing accountable approval and audit behavior. Require both cash and wallet differences to be checked and explained before completion. Never offset one against the other. Retain original counts, signed differences, earlier history, and the distinction between reviewed and recovered funds. Internal compatibility decisions must not rewrite old evidence merely to rename the action.
- Expose Administrator Emergency handover / Stop service using existing eligible-Shift custody controls, physical cash count, wallet check, and a required reason. With an eligible replacement, hand over custody; without one, safely stop new exchanges. Neither completes pending exchanges nor removes shortages. Incoming Cashiers explicitly accept individual unfinished exchanges; record acceptance, preserve the original recorder/movements, and expose unaccepted work as Needs a responsible Cashier. Responsible-Cashier follow-up must remain reachable beyond original-recorder/session filters.
- Replace cancellation's assumed automatic offset with actual return tracking. Record partial returns, required remaining cash/wallet/fee obligations as applicable, evidence, approvals, and responsible Cashier; leave failed exchanges pending until all required returns are actually completed and checked. Clicking Cancel is not evidence of movement. Preserve the original exchange and return history and prevent duplicate posting on repeated or competing confirmations.
- Corrections distinguish Fix a recording mistake (no money moved now) from Record an actual money movement. Preview effects, require an explanation, link to the original, and preserve applicable supervisor approval. Neither action initiates a GCash-app transfer. Historical recording corrections preserve original entries and closing counts and must not masquerade as present-day money. Actual later receipts/returns use the responsible Cashier's current open session and effective date; require opening an eligible session if none exists.
- Limit Product-drawer funding to the recording Cashier's own open drawer. Preview its specific Register/drawer and amount before approval. Funding uses one joint form capturing source, amount, eligible Administrator approval, and responsible Cashier confirmation that funds were actually received; post only after all are confirmed. Self-approval/receipt confirmation is allowed for a person assigned both roles. No cross-drawer transfer or pending funding-request subsystem is authorized.
- Add Money history within GCash Overview for additions, withdrawals, exchanges, returns, and corrections, including recording/approving actors and links. Clearly distinguish actual movements from recording corrections. Later recovery of an earlier reviewed shortage is an approved actual receipt in today's open session linked to the earlier review; retain original shortage and show later recovered amounts. A movement linked to both an exchange and discrepancy is counted once with multiple traceable links.
- Show Fees charged, Fees returned, and Fees after returns separately. Returns are explicit exchange-linked records, never inferred from arbitrary cash/wallet corrections. Charge fees on completion date and returns on actual-return date; the selected-period difference may be negative and must not be clamped. These are service-fee measures, not profit after expenses.
- Completed GCash activity and completed history use actual completion date. Unfinished/cancelled history uses start date with clear labels. Apply the effective date consistently to filtering, display, ordering, pagination, and the existing complete filtered exchange CSV. Retain start/completion dates in details where available; do not invent historical cancellation times or classify legacy reference-only review as completed exchange money. Preserve other existing shared/local filter scopes. Label the CSV exchange-only; Money history does not authorize a complete-ledger export.
- Supervisor approval uses the existing Login Identifier rules: Username or Email Address plus password. Require an enabled eligible ordinary Administrator assignment, including additional access, and preserve account/password-state checks, per-action limits, required reasons, audit attribution, and appropriate abuse protections. A dual-role person may approve their own eligible action. Record both Cashier and approving Administrator even when the same person. Super Administrator credentials alone are ineligible. Do not retain passwords between prompts, log in or switch the approver's session, grant general access, or create reusable approval sessions.
- Apply those common supervisor rules to drawer operations, applicable GCash actions, and exceptional refunds. The Cashier still records the operation on the Cashier screen; the credential prompt is a narrow approval exception to active-Workspace separation. Exceptional refunds retain two distinct approvals: access to that receipt, then fresh approval of the displayed selected-item/quantity refund amount. Preserve single-sale/Cashier/Shift binding, expiry, and existing own-sale behavior without supervisor credentials. Different eligible Administrators may approve the two stages; remove the same-approver requirement without weakening final authorization.
- Refund Correction Review preserves the original sale/refund and records the mistake, Administrator review/approval, and explicit required/completed correction steps. The Cashier executes actual money corrections through permitted Shift controls; the Inventory Manager executes separate stock corrections. A finished investigation may say Reviewed—correction outstanding, with remaining tasks visible to responsible roles. Only completed required corrections justify Corrected. Review approval itself moves neither money nor stock.
- Enforce the active Workspace in backend actions, routes, and visible controls. The Super Administrator Workspace provides read-only Store oversight, including GCash; operational mutations require the corresponding role already assigned and active. Technical identity is not an operational bypass. Preserve explicit shared Database Backup capability, protected Administrator restore, and technical model-maintenance authority. Independently preserve ADR-0019's read-only remote restrictions, including remote approvals, backups, and bulk exports.
- Ordinary staff lifecycle remains in the Administrator Workspace with fixed Administrator/Cashier/Inventory Manager assignments and existing protected-identity, self-action, password, and last-active-Administrator rules. Super Administrator ordinary-user authority is limited to creating ordinary Administrator accounts and disabling ordinary accounts with Administrator access; it grants no general Cashier-only/Inventory-Manager-only management, staff profile/password editing, arbitrary role assignment, or inferred re-enable permission. Preserve technical self-account security and Recovery Account boundaries.
- Permit immediate account disablement under existing self/last-Administrator protections despite unfinished cash work; revoke access and expose pending duties with permitted intervention links. Never auto-close/reconcile work. Role removal immediately withdraws its Workspace and revokes sessions while preserving unfinished duties. Explain whole-account/multi-role consequences. Revoke Sessions signs out current sessions and permits later eligible sign-in; password reset, session revocation, disablement, and operational closure remain distinct.
- During Administrator closure of an abandoned Shift, allow cancellation only of that Shift's unfinished unpaid Held Sales with a required reason and saver/canceller attribution. It creates no sale, refund, or cash movement and does not grant general access to operate another Cashier's carts.
- Operational Audit includes ordinary staff lifecycle events, including ordinary Administrators: creation, profile/role changes, enable/disable, and password-reset events, with actor/time and no passwords. Preserve technical-account security restrictions, Protected Audit Record immutability, and the same visibility scope in details, search, and exports. No audit editing/deletion is introduced.
- Promotion labels distinguish enabled from currently eligible. Describe explicit Cashier selection, not automatic or highest-discount selection, without adding edit/delete features. Register disabling blocks new Shifts and does not close/lock an existing one. Calendar Closed/Holiday describes Store/forecast context and does not gate checkout or close Shifts; retain Holiday's closed-trading-day forecasting meaning and use Open when trading on a holiday.
- Label Low Stock and Inventory Value Current stock and hide ineffective historical controls. Expiration reports disclose their actual Today-based scope rather than implying selected dates apply. Do not reconstruct historical inventory. Rename valuation's per-unit Profit column Price minus cost per item and preserve earned Gross Profit as a separate measure.
- Stock Value uses remaining batch quantities and recorded purchase costs, not latest Product cost for all units: ten units at PHP 5 plus ten at PHP 6 have Recorded Stock Value PHP 110. For missing purchase costs only, a usable positive current Product cost may supply an explicitly identified estimate. Show Recorded value, Estimated value, and quantities with Cost missing separately; label combined recorded-plus-estimated totals honestly and never present partial sums as complete exact value. Explicitly recorded zero batch/receiving cost is known zero; default zero catalog cost does not establish free goods. Flag stock-total/batch mismatches instead of estimating over unreliable quantities. Trace costing consumers without changing unrelated Gross Profit costing semantics.
- Inventory Manager reports are limited to inventory-related stock, receiving, expiry, inventory value, and permitted demand information. Exclude Sales by Cashier/staff financial performance, financial refund reports, and Gross Profit, including direct/export access. Preserve permitted receipt lookup for inventory work without extending it to full financial-report access.
- Align sales-report refunds with refund issue/payment date, consistent with Dashboard/Gross Profit. An October sale refunded in November retains October sales and records November refund effects, with the original-sale link. Preserve authoritative Gross Sales, Discounts, Refunds, Net Sales, and Gross Profit definitions; do not silently group normal period refunds by original sale date. Replace duplicate browser-print controls with Print / Save as PDF using the existing browser feature.
- Administrator forecasts expose operational results, readiness, origin/age, and generation status, distinguishing ready, updating, old, and unavailable. Ordinary Store forecast generation follows Administrator operational authority; preserve permitted Inventory Manager demand access. Technical training/testing/comparison scores, configuration, approval, and rollback remain with Super Administrator. Do not add a replacement misleading forecast-versus-sales comparison. Show unavailable confidence as unavailable rather than zero and count distinct Products where labels say Products.
- Protected restore guidance shows durable current stage and next action, including waiting for technical approval, ready to restore, verification needed, and actionable failure/recovery states. Preserve approved encrypted-package/passphrase, destination protection, Identity Reconciliation/Reconciliation Approval, safety-copy, replacement warning, write pause, automated/human verification, and explicit reopening requirements. Another authorized Administrator may take over finishing checks/reopening using their own verified password, with takeover attribution and all prerequisites intact; never reopen automatically or infer bypass authority from account availability.
- Retain accessible labels, keyboard/dialog behavior, existing mobile layouts, formula-safe complete filtered CSV, accurate role-specific links, safe Operator Alerts, and failure-safe data states. Do not expose raw internal errors in Store-facing failures or turn missing data into zero/all clear. Preserve request freshness behavior so stale responses cannot replace newer selections. Detailed diagnostics remain in existing protected logs as applicable.
- Persist only the additional review history, closure-threshold evidence, responsibility acceptance, actual-return/correction links, fee-return attribution, refund task evidence, and recovery takeover evidence needed to satisfy these behaviors, extending existing records where practical. Exact schema and route shapes are implementation choices, not a license for parallel ledgers or destructive historical rewrites. Maintain atomic posting and existing concurrency protections across affected entry points.

## Testing Decisions

- User-confirmed seams: existing workflow/service entry points against disposable databases, HTTP/request checks for permissions and forged requests, and focused browser checks of changed screens. Prefer the highest existing seam that exercises the behavior. Add no shared facade, test framework, production-only test interface, or live Store fixture.
- Good tests exercise externally visible results: returned/rendered data, persisted balances/stock, immutable originals, review/task states, actor/time attribution, notifications, CSV contents, and unchanged state after refusal. Avoid assertions about private helpers, incidental CSS, SQL text, or implementation source strings.
- Prior art includes Administrator Dashboard workspace/route/browser tests; GCash transaction integration, Administrator approval contract, and overview/browser coverage; Cashier Shift reconciliation and Held Sale contracts; Cash Refund contract/concurrency and safe-refund browser tests; audit visibility integration; staff minimum/management and Workspace-switching tests; Gross Profit historical-cost/reporting and inventory export tests; and recovery authentication/restart/reopening integration/browser suites. Reuse their existing disposable fixtures and application boundaries; update obsolete expectations rather than preserve retired behavior for tests.
- Cash review: exercise positive/negative/zero differences and below/equal/above limits, including a zero limit with zero difference, exact-Shift deep links outside dates, independent draft notes, required final explanations, missing-money conclusions, review reopening/history, threshold increases/decreases, and unchanged closing evidence. Required reviews must remain pending after later Store/platform limit changes.
- Attention: exercise global GCash counts outside selected date/Cashier filters, hide-on-known-zero, unavailable and partial-failure states, post-action refresh, normal versus overdue/high-value Stock Issues, correct role destinations, and complete removal of discount/reversal alerts, notification producers, obsolete counts, and settings consumers without lost underlying history.
- GCash custody/follow-up: cover replacement and no-replacement emergency outcomes, eligibility failures, cash/wallet checks, individual acceptance/refusal, unassigned items, original attribution, responsible-Cashier access after session/Shift/period closure, and disappearance of settled obligations from current pending totals without altering historical carryover evidence.
- GCash money: cover partial and complete returns, uncertain completion evidence, independent cash/wallet differences, no automatic cancellation offsets, repeated/concurrent submissions, current-session requirements, absent-session refusal, historical no-movement corrections, approved funding with actual-receipt confirmation, own-drawer restrictions, shortage recovery, and one movement linked to multiple records without double counting. Refused actions must leave money/history unchanged.
- Dates/fees: use exchanges started and completed on different Philippine dates/months, pending/cancelled/legacy records, mixed Cashiers, partial fee returns in later periods, negative Fees after returns, consistent effective-date list/order/pagination/CSV, and retained original dates. Verify all matching export pages, formula-injection protection, and honest exchange-only labels.
- Supervisor/refunds: test Username and Email Address, primary/additional Administrator assignment, allowed self-approval, technical-only rejection, disabled/invalid/password-state accounts, abuse protections, required reasons, and no approver-session mutation or retained password.
