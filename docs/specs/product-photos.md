# Product photos

Confirmed 2026-10-29.

Product photos help staff identify goods in the Cashier POS product cards and cart, Inventory product list and details/edit preview, and Stock Receiving product selection.

Missing photos and failed image requests show the same box placeholder. Product names remain visible and photo failures do not prevent selling or receiving. Photos fit within their frames without cropping the packaging.

Uploaded photos remain in protected storage and are delivered through an authenticated endpoint. No changes to receipts or reports are required.
