# RetailMind Landing Page

## Mode

Persuade.

## User outcome

A Shalom Store staff member should understand that RetailMind connects stock, sales, forecasting, and replenishment, then open the secure workspace without learning a second visual language.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The public entrance is the first room of the same operational system, not a separate campaign. It refuses the green industrial poster treatment and presents RetailMind as a calm, role-aware workspace.

**OWN-WORLD:** Control Navy structure, Action Blue interaction, cool Worksurface Grey, white panels, thin grey rules, 12–14px radii, quiet depth, compact status signals, and system UI typography.

**STORY:** The visitor sees the Store workflow as one connected record, understands the main operational capabilities, follows the sale-to-replenishment sequence, and chooses Staff login.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A white app-style header sits above a two-column hero on the cool canvas. The left column carries the promise and blue action; the right is a bordered inventory-desk panel with a compact four-stage operational ledger. A full-width process rail closes the viewport.

**FORM:** Workspace preview, chosen directly from the established role-based visual world. No concept seed is required because the user fixed both the identity and the target relationship.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
