Mode: Read. Audience: Store staff and Administrators reading transactional email on phones and desktop inboxes.

Scope: shared Brevo frame, password reset, Administrator welcome, Daily Stock Brief, and generic notification bodies. Existing content, delivery, recipient rules, logo URL and security behavior remain intact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A compact Store letter makes the reason for the email immediately readable. Replace the tall header, stacked outlined cards and oversized footer with one calm reading sheet.

OWN-WORLD: Existing RetailMind image logo, white paper on cool slate ground, blue for the reset code and primary emphasis, near-black headings, slate body text. Email-safe system sans, restrained rules, no decorative imagery or motion.

STORY: Identify RetailMind and the Store, understand the message, then use the code or credentials or scan the report. Safety guidance stays beside the sensitive content.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 600px sheet with a compact masthead: 156px logo left and Store name right. A 24px heading and 15px body follow on a shared inset. A blue-tinted horizontal code band or flat credential rows carry essential details. Stock counts share one summary strip above readable tables. The quiet footer sits below the sheet.
