How profit is calculated

Gross Sales: Sales before discounts and refunds.

Net Sales: Gross Sales minus discounts and cash refunds.

Gross Profit: Net Sales minus product cost, adjusted for returns. Gross Profit excludes operating expenses (Rent, wages, electricity).

Restockable returns: Reverse Net Sales and Product Cost.

Unsaleable returns: Reverse Net Sales; product cost remains an inventory loss.
