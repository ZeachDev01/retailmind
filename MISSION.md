# Mission: Explain RetailMind during a sari-sari Store demo

## Why
Understand how daily Store operations connect so the client can follow the current RetailMind iteration and give useful feedback during the visit.

## Success looks like
- Explain how receiving, checkout, inventory, and cashier cash records connect.
- Demonstrate one sale and identify its effect on stock and expected cash.
- Explain who handles exceptions and replenishment without promising unverified behavior.

## Constraints
- Focus requested: how daily Store operations connect.
- Keep lessons short, concrete, and grounded in the current repository.
- Distinguish illustrative examples from actual client records and live verification.

## Out of scope
No explicit exclusions yet; start with daily operations before technical architecture.
